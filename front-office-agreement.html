This is the agreement between ALWIZE LLC (“Alwize”), ask@getalwize.com, getalwize.com, and the business that applies for Alwize Front Office (the “Client”). The Client signs it in the Front Office Launch Form, which fills in the Client’s business name, contact and chosen plan.

1. Overview

Alwize will provide Alwize Front Office to the Client: a virtual receptionist that answers the Client’s business phone line, and on the Plus plan its text messages, in the Client’s business name. It answers callers from information the Client approves, books appointments into the Client’s Front Office calendar, sends booking confirmations by text, transfers callers to the Client during business hours, and takes voicemails when no one is available. The Client sees every call, text, booking and voicemail in the Front Office dashboard at app.getalwize.com.

3. Approval and setup

  • Application. The Client applies through the Front Office Launch Form. Alwize reviews every application and may decline any business, including businesses in industries the phone carriers do not allow to send business texts.
  • Payment after approval. No payment is taken until Alwize approves the application.
  • Setup. Alwize provides a business phone number, loads the Client’s approved answers, hours and closed dates, and runs a test call with the Client before the line goes live.
  • Texting registration (Plus). Every business that sends texts must be registered with the US phone carriers. Alwize submits this on the Client’s behalf. Approval usually takes two to three weeks and is decided by the carriers, not Alwize. Calls can go live before texting is approved. If the carriers reject the registration, the Client may move to Essentials or cancel.

4. Client responsibilities

The Client will:

  • Keep its business information, hours, closed dates, transfer number and approved answers accurate and up to date in the dashboard. The receptionist only says what the Client has approved.
  • Answer, or arrange cover for, transferred calls during business hours, and review voicemails and flagged conversations promptly.
  • Only use Front Office for lawful business purposes, and not to sell, promote or discuss anything the phone carriers prohibit (for example cannabis, firearms, high-risk lending, gambling or adult content).
  • Only text people who have contacted the business first or have agreed to receive texts, honor every STOP request, and not send marketing texts through Front Office.
  • Give callers any notice that the law in its state or industry requires, including that calls are answered by an automated receptionist.
  • Not ask callers to share medical, financial-account or other sensitive information through Front Office.

5. Limits of the service, calls and data

  • Automated service. Front Office is an automated receptionist. Speech recognition can mishear callers, and calls, texts or bookings can occasionally be missed or recorded incorrectly. The Client should review its calendar and conversations regularly.
  • Not for emergencies. Front Office is not an emergency service and does not replace 911 or any urgent line. It does not give medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Availability. Alwize aims to keep Front Office running at all times but does not guarantee uninterrupted service. It depends on third-party providers, including phone carriers.
  • Liability. Alwize is not liable for lost business, missed or incorrect bookings, or indirect losses. Alwize’s total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Client paid in the month before the claim.
  • What is stored. Front Office stores call transcripts, text messages, bookings, caller names and numbers, and voicemail recordings with their transcripts. Live calls are transcribed but not recorded. This information belongs to the Client; Alwize uses it only to provide the service, as set out in the Privacy Policy.
  • Healthcare. Front Office is not yet set up for protected health information. Medical practices may not use it until Alwize confirms otherwise in writing.

6. Phone number, cancellation and other terms

  • Phone number. Alwize provides the Front Office number. If the Client cancels with its account paid up, Alwize will help move the number to the Client’s own phone provider on request. Unclaimed numbers are released 30 days after service ends.
  • Cancellation. Either party may cancel with 30 days’ written notice by email. The Client is billed through the end of the notice period, and payments already made are not refunded. Alwize may suspend or end the service sooner if the Client breaks the texting rules or this agreement, or does not pay.
  • Changes. Alwize will give at least 30 days’ notice by email before changing prices or plan allowances.
  • Terms and privacy. This agreement includes Alwize’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. If this agreement conflicts with the Terms of Service, this agreement controls. It is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

7. Acceptance

The Client accepts this agreement by checking the acceptance box and typing its authorized signer’s full name in the Launch Form, or by paying the first invoice. Alwize records the signer’s name, the date and time, and this agreement’s version with the application.
